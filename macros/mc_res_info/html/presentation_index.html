Joint AllGains NSC fits: Cali and NoCali

Each plot shows the AllGains SRO and DRO resolution points in EB and EE, fitted separately with one shared NSC curve for that calibration state. The lower panel shows each point's pull from the curve. Black crosses mark points excluded by the 2.5 peer-pull filter. The two states use the same MC sample with and without the timing calibration; the fitted Cali curve is about 11% higher across the shared x range. This is a descriptive comparison: the fit errors shown do not include the covariance between the two processing states.
